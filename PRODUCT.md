# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, founders, and technical decision-makers reviewing Ferdinand Macagba's profile under time pressure. They need to understand quickly what he builds, how he works, how credible the evidence is, and whether his AI-assisted development approach is mature enough for a business context.

## Product Purpose

This digital profile exists to position Ferdinand as a human-led, AI-assisted application developer and digital product builder. Success means the page creates trust, invites deeper reading, and prepares better interview or client conversations around product judgment, responsible AI, deployed work, and operational maturity.

## Brand Personality

Executive, intelligent, exacting. The profile should feel like a serious operator's dossier: considered, modern, calm, and specific without becoming sterile or generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-generated portfolio tropes: purple-blue gradients, glowing blobs, repetitive card grids, tiny uppercase labels above every section, exaggerated claims, gimmicky developer aesthetics, and template SaaS styling. Avoid implying traditional hand-coding expertise where the honest position is AI-assisted direction, review, and delivery.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with judgment, not decoration.
2. Make the first scan answer the hiring question: what does Ferdinand build, how does he work, and why should I trust him?
3. Use color as an authority system: restraint for credibility, accent only where it guides attention.
4. Show responsible AI as operating discipline, not as buzzword copy.
5. Keep every claim defensible in an interview.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA or better for text contrast, visible keyboard focus, readable line lengths, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion accommodations. Color should support hierarchy but never be the only carrier of meaning.
