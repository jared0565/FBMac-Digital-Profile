# Design

## Visual Theme

Executive intelligence dossier. The page should feel like a calm operating brief for hiring decision-makers: precise, high-contrast, information-rich, and visually memorable without decorative excess.

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Use | Psychology |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Obsidian | `#07090d` | Body background, hero field | Seriousness, focus, reduced visual noise |
| Graphite | `#10151d` | Main surfaces | Professional depth without pure-black harshness |
| Elevated Graphite | `#171f2a` | Secondary panels, controls | Layering and hierarchy |
| Rule Slate | `#2a3441` | Borders, dividers, structure | Control, precision, scannability |
| Warm Ivory | `#f4efe6` | Primary text | Human warmth and premium editorial contrast |
| Mist | `#b8c0cc` | Supporting text | Readable restraint, less brittle than gray |
| Brass | `#c9a45e` | Primary calls to action, authority accents | Executive confidence, value, achievement |
| Instrument Cyan | `#6bc7c4` | Responsible AI, systems, validation signals | Intelligence, clarity, technical calm |

## Typography

- Display: Playfair Display, retained from the existing brand for name recognition and personal-brand warmth.
- Body: Inter, retained for interface readability and deployment stability.
- Headings use balanced wrapping to avoid awkward breaks.
- Body copy uses readable line length and high contrast.

## Layout

- First viewport uses an asymmetric dossier layout: identity and CTA on the left, hiring brief on the right.
- Subtle grid texture replaces decorative glow blobs.
- Cards use 8px radius or less for a more professional profile surface.
- Section labels are title-case and quiet, not repeated all-caps scaffolding.

## Components

- CTAs are solid, restrained, and action-specific.
- Project cards keep clear problem/solution/technical evidence structure.
- Responsible AI content uses cyan as a system-control signal.
- Contact cards are limited to Email, Phone, and LinkedIn.

## Accessibility

- Target WCAG AA or better for normal text.
- Do not rely on color alone for meaning.
- Keep focus styles visible.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
